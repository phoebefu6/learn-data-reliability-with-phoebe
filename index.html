Learn Data Reliability with Phoebe

Every reliability conversation that goes badly goes badly for the same reason: somebody made a promise before anybody made a measurement. Harborlight's team believed their orders pipeline was basically 99 percent. Counted by partition it was 95.14, and the difference is a month of promises the history could not keep. This course builds the contract properly: indicators for partitions, a target the history supports, an error budget that decides what ships, alerting that pages once per incident instead of thirty-five times, and the runbooks, backfills and postmortems that make a rota survivable. Six leader sessions on the promise, the budget and the humans; eight builder sessions on one pipeline, measured, promised, alerted, documented and replayed over thirty nights on call.

What is real here 🧾

A course about promises should be exact about which of its own numbers are measured.
